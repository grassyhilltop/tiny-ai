## The AI Fluency framing

Lab 1 frames itself as a rep of **AI fluency** and closes by naming the four competencies the
reader just exercised. That framework is not ours.

**The AI Fluency Framework** and its **4D** competencies — Delegation, Description, Discernment,
Diligence — were developed by **Prof. Rick Dakan** (Ringling College of Art and Design) and
**Prof. Joseph Feller** (Cork University Business School, University College Cork), in
collaboration with Anthropic.

- The authors' own home for it: <https://aifluencyframework.org/>
- The free course, *AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations*, taught by Dakan and Feller with
  **Maggie Vo** and **Drew Bent** of Anthropic: <https://www.anthropic.com/ai-fluency/overview>

## The AI Fluency Index

The "production up, discernment down" idea on the page comes from the **Anthropic Education
Report: The AI Fluency Index** (Feb 2026): <https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index>

- **Written by Kristen Swanson**, who designed the research and led the analysis.
- **Zoe Ludwig** and **Drew Bent** contributed framework alignment, messaging and review.
- Also credited: Zack Lee (technical support), Hanah Ho (data visualisation), and Keir Bradwell,
  Rebecca Hiscott, Ryan Donegan and Sarah Pollack (communications review).

**Two things to keep straight, because both are easy to get wrong:**

1. The report was **not** "rewritten by Zoe Ludwig" — Kristen Swanson wrote it. Crediting Ludwig as
   its author would misstate someone else's work.
2. **"The artifact paradox" is not the report's phrase** and does not appear in it. The report's
   own section heading is *"When creating outputs, users become more directive but less
   evaluative."* Anthropic's companion discussion guide calls the idea **the artifact effect**.
   The page quotes the report's wording and does not attribute the coinage.

The report is explicit that this is an association, not a cause; the page says so too.
The finding rests on artifact-producing conversations showing higher directive behaviour — goal
clarification +14.7pp, format +14.5pp, examples +13.4pp, iteration +9.7pp against non-artifact
conversations — while all three discernment behaviours fall.
