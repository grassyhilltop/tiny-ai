`codon.html` is a verbatim copy of https://github.com/grassyhilltop/codon (index.html).
It is embedded by the labs in an iframe and driven over postMessage. To update, copy the
file across again; do not edit it here, or the two will drift.

On the vocabulary. codon is a visual programming language for CS novices. Its terms — cell,
wire, gate, synapse, axon, inhibition — are borrowed metaphor, chosen to give a beginner a
concrete picture; neither codon nor this site is a biology project. One scene in the source
draws the lac operon, as a stress test of whether inhibition can be a glyph rather than a
word; it is not in the tab list, so you will only meet it by reading the file, and codon's
own note on it says nothing there is a biology feature — it is cells, wires and one minus
sign. Because this file is a verbatim copy, do not rewrite that vocabulary here: change it
upstream in the codon repo and copy across again.
